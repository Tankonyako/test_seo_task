## Is online gambling legal in Canada?

Yes, but the rules depend on your province. Canada leaves gambling to the provinces, so there isn’t one national answer.

*   Ontario has a regulated, open market. Private casinos must be registered with iGaming Ontario, and Ontario players should stick to those registered sites.
*   Most other provinces run their own government site, such as PlayNow in British Columbia and Manitoba, Loto-Québec in Quebec and PlayAlberta in Alberta. Alberta has also passed a law to open a regulated market to private operators, so check the latest status if you live there.
*   Many Canadians outside Ontario also play at offshore casinos licensed elsewhere, such as Kahnawake, Malta or Curaçao. Players are rarely prosecuted for this, but these sites aren’t regulated by your province, so choosing a reputable operator matters even more.

The minimum age is 19 in most provinces and 18 in Alberta, Manitoba and Quebec.
