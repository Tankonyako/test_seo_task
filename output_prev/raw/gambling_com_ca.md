**Our 10 Golden Rules for Responsible Gambling**

Follow these key principles to stay in control, minimise risk and enjoy a safer, more balanced gambling experience:

**1\. Set Limits and Stick to Them**  
Decide your budget in advance – and only gamble what you can afford to lose.

**2\. Take Regular Breaks**  
Avoid long, uninterrupted sessions. Take time to reset and regain perspective.

**3\. Never Chase Losses**  
Trying to win back money can lead to bigger losses. Accept them as part of the risk.

**4\. Stay Clear-Headed**  
Don’t gamble when you're drunk, angry, or emotional – it clouds your judgement.

**5\. Keep Gambling in Balance**  
Make sure gambling doesn’t interfere with your work, relationships, or daily life.

**6\. Track Your Gambling Activity**  
Keep a clear record of the time and money you spend to stay aware and accountable.

**7\. Bet With Knowledge**  
Whatever you're betting on, understand the odds, rules and risks before you start.

**8\. Manage Your Bankroll**  
Split your bankroll into smaller units and stake only a small percentage per bet. Adjust as you go.

**9\. Maintain Other Interests**  
Gambling should complement your lifestyle – not replace other hobbies or priorities.
