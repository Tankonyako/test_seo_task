## Is It Legal to Gamble at Online Casinos in Canada?

**Short answer**: yes. Playing at an internationally licensed online casino is legal for Canadians, and that's true whether or not your province regulates its own market.

Canada doesn't licence online casinos at the federal level, and — outside Ontario's AGCO-regulated market and Alberta's incoming AGLC-regulated market — no province has built its own private licensing regime either. Nothing in Canadian law makes it an offence for a player to use an internationally licensed site, and in practice, enforcement against individual players simply doesn't happen. 

Players in British Columbia, Quebec, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Atlantic Canada, and the territories legally play in two ways: through their province's own lottery-run platform (like PlayNow or Espacejeux), or at a casino licensed elsewhere — most commonly by the Kahnawake Gaming Commission (based in Quebec), the Malta Gaming Authority, or the UK Gambling Commission.

That said, **legal isn't the same as regulated**, and I think that distinction is worth being upfront about. I only recommend sites holding a recognised licence such as Kahnawake or Malta, because that licence means the casino has passed background checks, uses independently audited RNGs, and can be held accountable if it treats you unfairly.
