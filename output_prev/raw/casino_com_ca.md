## Gambling Regulations in Canada: Are Online Casinos Legal?

While online gambling is legal nationwide under the Criminal Code of Canada, there are complexities players need to know about. The federal government does not directly regulate gambling, instead leaving it up to individual provinces and territories. This allows for separate regulated online gambling platforms, some government-run (British Columbia, Manitoba, Quebec) and some offering licenses to private online casinos (Ontario).  

Outside of Ontario, private online casinos cannot legally operate in any province or territory. But players are able to visit casino sites that have a license from an international gambling authority.  

In 2022, Ontario became the first Canadian province to license and regulate online casinos. As of July 2025, the province is home to over 70 online casinos that operate under licenses from the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario (AGCO) and its subsidiary iGaming Ontario (iGO). These casinos are only accessible to those who are physically located in the province; anyone who tries to access Ontario casinos from outside the province will be blocked.
