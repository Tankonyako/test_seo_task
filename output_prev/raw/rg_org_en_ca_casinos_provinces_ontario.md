### Ontario Online Gambling Laws & Safety – Is Online Casino Gambling Legal in Ontario?

### Is online casino gambling legal in Ontario?

Yes. Ontario is currently the only province in Canada with a fully regulated online casino market open to private operators. Online casino gambling is legal in Ontario, but only on regulated casino sites that are approved at the provincial level. This legal framework applies to all online casino games, including slots, live dealer games, blackjack, and other popular table games. In addition to 70 international casino sites, Ontarians may also choose the provincially operated “OLG,” in operation since 1975, which celebrated 50 years of gaming in June 2025.

#### When Was Online Casino Gambling Legalized in Ontario?

The ON casino market launched on April 4, 2022, when the Government of Ontario formally opened its regulated online gambling and gaming market to private operators. Since that date, online casinos may legally offer casino games to Ontario players only if they are:

*   Registered with the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario (AGCO)
*   Operating under a commercial agreement with iGaming Ontario (iGO)

### What law regulates online casinos in Ontario?

Ontario’s online casino market is regulated through a combination of provincial legislation and modern internet gaming rules.

The legal foundation is the Gaming Control Act, 1992, which gives the province authority to regulate gambling activities and enforce compliance with its regulations. While the Act predates online casinos, it provides the statutory basis for Ontario’s regulated gaming market.

Operational rules for online casinos are set by the Registrar’s Standards for Internet Gaming, issued by the Alcohol and Gaming Commission. These standards govern how casinos offer games, verify players, process deposits and payouts, advertise, and provide customer support.

Finally, iGaming Ontario (iGO) manages the Operating Agreement framework, allowing approved private operators to legally offer online casino games in the province.

These frameworks define how online casinos operate in Ontario and give AGCO and iGaming Ontario the authority to enforce rules, issue penalties, or remove non-compliant sites from the regulated market.

### What is the legal gambling age in Ontario?

The legal age to play online casino games in Ontario is **19+**.  
This applies to:

*   Online casino sites
*   Land-based casinos
*   Live dealer games and digital slots

All regulated casinos are required to enforce age verification as part of their customer onboarding and ongoing compliance.

### Are regulated Ontario online casinos safe?

Yes. Regulated Ontario casino sites are designed to meet high standards of safety and fairness.

Approved casinos must:

*   Use independently tested RNG systems
*   Display accurate RTP information
*   Protect player data and funds

*   Provide accessible customer support and complaint-resolution processes

Because these sites operate under provincial oversight, issues related to payouts, payments, or unfair game behavior can be escalated through formal regulatory channels.

### Can Ontario players use offshore or international casino sites?

No. Ontario does not treat offshore casinos as a grey market. Online casino sites must be registered with the AGCO and authorized by iGaming Ontario to offer gambling in the province legally. Casinos that operate under international licenses are not permitted to operate in Ontario, regardless of their reputation or licensing status outside the province. This is a key distinction between Ontario and neighboring provinces such as Quebec and Manitoba, where players may still encounter legally accessible offshore casino options under different provincial frameworks. 

### Are gambling winnings taxed in Ontario?

In Ontario and across Canada, online casino winnings are generally not taxed when gambling is carried out for personal entertainment.

According to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), gambling winnings become taxable only in exceptional cases where a player is considered to be carrying on a gambling business. This is a high bar and is determined on a case-by-case basis, not by a single factor such as frequency or size of winnings.

The CRA and Canadian courts have consistently ruled that even regular, frequent, and systematic gambling does not automatically constitute a business. Most casino play — including online slots, blackjack, live dealer games, and table games — is treated as a game of chance, not a commercial activity.

Winnings may be taxable **only if** the gambling activity shows clear business characteristics, such as:

*   A high degree of organization and structure
*   Use of **special skill, insider knowledge, or information** to reduce chance
*   Gambling is conducted as a **primary source of livelihood**
*   Evidence of commercial intent beyond normal recreational play

These situations are **rare** and typically involve activities very different from standard online casino play.

What it means for you

For the vast majority of Ontario players using online casinos recreationally, winnings are not taxable, and losses are not deductible. Taxation applies only in exceptional circumstances where gambling crosses into business activity under CRA standards.
