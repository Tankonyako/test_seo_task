## What is a No Deposit Bonus?

A no deposit bonus is a casino promotion that gives players free spins or bonus funds **without requiring a deposit**. Frequently, no deposit bonuses are given to new players as a way to try games without financial risk. However, some casinos also include no deposit elements in their broader welcome packages, or regular promotions which may be accessible to existing users.

## How Do Casino No Deposit Bonuses Work?

No deposit bonuses come in various forms - bonus cash, free spins, or time-limited play. Some require you to input a bonus code during sign-up or in the cashier section, however many are added to your account following bonus acceptance.  

Game restrictions and expiry dates may also apply. No deposit bonuses always come with wagering requirements - typically 30x to 45x the bonus amount - and a maximum cashout cap, so read the T&Cs carefully before playing to understand how much you can actually withdraw.

## No Deposit Bonuses Terms & Conditions

As with all casino bonuses, no deposit offers have a list of T&Cs and criteria that need to be met. 

Here are the most common:

*   **Wagering Requirements** \- also known as playthrough requirements, these are typically applied to no deposit offers. Although no deposit is needed to claim the bonus, you must still wager the bonus amount a set number of times before withdrawing any winnings. 
*   **Maximum Bet Amount** \- No deposit bonuses usually have a maximum bet limit. If you bet over the limit, you risk losing the bonus.
*   **Win Limit** \- Similarly to the maximum bet amount, no deposit bonuses will usually have a cap on winnings - typically quite a low amount compared to a deposit bonus. This means that anything over the top amount will not be withdrawable. 
*   **Game Restriction** \- Live casino games and progressive jackpot slots are almost universally excluded from no deposit bonus play. The bonus is typically restricted to a specific selection of standard online slots so check the eligible games list before registering.
*   **Bonus Expiry** - No deposit bonuses are only valid for a limited time once claimed - typically around 7 days. If you don’t use the bonus within this window, it will expire, and any winnings tied to it will be lost.

\***Game Contributions -** Something to be mindful when completing wagering requirements. Not all games contribute equally toward clearing wagering requirements. Slots typically count 100% while table games like blackjack and roulette often contribute just 10% or less, if at all. You will find a list of exempt games within the T&Cs so be sure to check this before depositing. 

## Types of No Deposit Bonuses

Canadian casinos offer different kinds of no deposit bonuses. From bonus spins on a specific slot game to no deposit cash bonuses, you can find all the following options:

*    [img: Free Play] 

    ### Free Play

    A free play offer will grant you a bonus amount to use on selected games. To use this form of no deposit offer, you'll often need to enter a promotional code while creating an account and navigate to a particular game to enjoy some play for free.

*    [img: Free Spins] 

    ### Free Spins

    A free spins no deposit bonus is the most common. It can be broken down into two main types - regular free spins and no wagering spins. The former come with a wagering requirement on any winnings, while the latter pay out instant cash with no need to wager. The wagering requirement can be fulfilled by playing online slots, but the free spins can only be used on certain slot games.

*    [img: Free Cash] 

    ### Free Cash

    Some sites prefer free cash to free play. Rather than limiting you to a specific title, these no deposit offers give you free rounds on a selection of games, though any winnings will be subject to a wagering requirement and maximum bet restrictions may apply.

*    [img: Cashback] 

    ### Cashback

    A few sites - mainly new online casinos - offer no deposit cashback, but it's still uncommon. You get a portion of your losses back, up to a set limit. It’s a useful fallback, though the bonus usually expires quickly.

## No Deposit Bonuses FAQs

### Are no deposit bonuses legit and safe?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Yes, no deposit bonuses are legitimate as long as you claim them from a licensed casino. That said, always read the terms and conditions before claiming - wagering requirements, maximum cashout limits, and eligible games can vary a lot between offers. Sticking to casinos we've reviewed and ranked is the easiest way to avoid any bonuses with unfair or hidden terms.

### How do I claim a no deposit bonus?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Claiming a no deposit bonus is usually quick and easy to do. First, sign up for an account at the casino offering the bonus. Next, verify your email or phone number if asked to do so. If a bonus code is required, enter it during registration or in the promotions section of your account. Once verified, the bonus should be automatically credited to your account.

### Do you need a bonus code for a no deposit bonus?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Not always. Some no deposit bonuses apply automatically as soon as you create an account, while others require a bonus code. If a code is needed, make sure to enter it correctly before completing your sign-up, as it usually can't be added after your account has been created. Check the terms of each offer to confirm details.

### What is the best no deposit bonus?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Our curated list of Canadian casinos highlights the best no deposit casino bonuses available. We carefully review and compare free money and free spins promotions to bring you the most valuable offers. Currently, 7Bit and Spinmama stand out as our top choices for no deposit bonuses.

### Can you use multiple no deposit bonuses?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Most casinos allow only one no deposit bonus per player. However, some offers combine free spins and bonus cash into a single package. In some cases, no deposit bonuses are linked to follow-up deposit bonuses that unlock even more perks, like extra free spins or matched deposit percentages for added value.

### Can you cash out no deposit bonus bet winnings?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Yes, you certainly can! However, cashing out no deposit bonus winnings is typically subject to wagering requirements, withdrawal limits, and other terms. In most cases, you’ll also need to make at least one real-money deposit before you are eligible to withdraw any winnings earned from the bonus.

### What are wagering requirements and playthrough requirements?

 [img: Down Arrow]  [img: Up Arrow] 

Wagering requirements or playthrough requirements are how many times a player will need to re-wager their winnings before they can withdraw it as cash. For example, if the wagering/playthrough requirements are 35x then a player will need to wager their bonus money 35 times before they can withdraw it.
