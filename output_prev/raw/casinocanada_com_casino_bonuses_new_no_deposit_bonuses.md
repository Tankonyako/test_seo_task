## FAQs

Do I always need a bonus code to claim the offer?

Not always. Some casinos credit your free spins or bonus money automatically after registration, while other new casinos with a no deposit require bonus codes. When they do, we let you know and list all the required bonus codes on our site. Always read the bonus terms to avoid missing a reward.

What is the difference between free spins and a cash chip?

Free spins are usually awarded in a certain number and are tied to a specific slot. Cash chips provide a bit more flexibility and allow you to use them across multiple games until they run out.

Why are no-deposit bonuses limited in terms of winnings?

No-deposit bonuses let Canadian players test a casino with no risk, so operators set caps to limit payouts. A new no deposit casino bonus usually allows withdrawals of C$50-C$100 and comes with wagering requirements (30x-50x), but this varies greatly by platform.

Our team makes sure that a no-deposit bonus comes from a licensed and regulated operator. In addition, we always check the bonus terms and withdrawal process. We confirm this by registering real accounts, redeeming the bonus, and testing payouts before listing any offer as verified.

Can I claim more than one no-deposit bonus at a time?

No, all new Canadian casinos with a no deposit bonus restrict new players to a single bonus per account, household, or IP address. However, you can still claim multiple offers by registering at different platforms and claiming the bonuses individually.
