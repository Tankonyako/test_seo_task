Now that you know what no deposit bonuses are and how they work, let's look at some common beginner mistakes you should avoid when using the bonus.

The wagering requirements are crucial when playing with a no deposit casino bonus. You should also check the bonus validity period, during which you must fulfill the wagering requirement.  Failure to fulfil the wagering requirement within the stipulated period can result in forfeiture of the bonus, plus its associated winnings.

### Withdrawing Too Early

You should never withdraw your no deposit bonus until you have fulfilled the wagering requirement. ## Wagering Requirements and Terms to Know

As mentioned, no deposit bonuses let you play for free and win real money. To benefit from these bonuses, however, you need to understand their terms and conditions, as every bonus comes with strict rules you must follow when using the bonus.

The wagering requirement is the most crucial term to pay close attention to before and after claiming the bonus. You can find it indicated as a multiplier on the bonus description, and it displays how many times you need to wager the bonus before you can claim the winnings. Say you sign up at a top-tier no-deposit casino in Canada and you get a C$10 no deposit bonus with a minimum wagering requirement of 30X. What this means is that you would have to wager the C$10 bonus at least 30 times before you can cash out winnings from the bonus. We recommend avoiding bonuses with unreasonably high wagering requirements. If you'd rather sidestep this altogether, our **guide to no-wagering casino bonuses** covers offers that skip the requirement entirely.

Other no-deposit bonus terms you need to keep an eye on include cash-out limits. The limits are usually small for no deposit bonuses, so you might need to compare deals at various casinos to get a reasonable offer. A key tip is to consider whether the limit is justified by the time you will need to play to fulfil the wagering requirement. If it’s not ideal, just move on to the next offer. In addition, you should check out the expiry date attached to the bonus as well as the game restrictions.
