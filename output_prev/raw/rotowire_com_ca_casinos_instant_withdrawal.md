## What Is KYC and How It Impacts Redemption Times

KYC stands for "**Know Your Customer"**. It is the identity verification process every regulated and reputable casino uses before releasing funds to a player for the first time. It exists to prevent fraud, money laundering, and underage gambling, and it is a legal requirement for licensed operators in Ontario and Alberta.

**What you will need to submit**

The documents required will **vary slightly by operator** but the core set is consistent across Canadian casinos:

*   Government-issued photo ID (**passport or driver's license**)
*   **Proof of address** dated within the last 3 months.
*   Payment method verification (a photo of the **card used to deposit**, or a screenshot of your e-wallet account showing your name)

Some operators also request a selfie holding your ID, particularly offshore casinos processing larger withdrawal amounts.

**How long it takes**

KYC reviews at licensed Ontario operators are **typically completed within 24 to 48 hours**, but could be longer. Offshore operators in other provinces vary more widely, with some clearing same-day and others taking up to 3 business days. Submitting clear, legible documents the first time around is the **single biggest factor in how fast it clears**. Blurry photos or expired documents restart the clock and are an easy way to delay your withdraw.

**How to get it out of the way**

Submit your KYC documents **immediately after creating your account**, before you make your first deposit. Most casinos allow this and it means your first withdrawal processes without the verification delay. If you wait until you are ready to cash out, you are adding **1 to 3 business days** to a process that could have already been done.

**After KYC clears**

Verification is a one-time process per casino. Once your account is verified, **future withdrawals skip the review queue entirely** and move straight to processing.
