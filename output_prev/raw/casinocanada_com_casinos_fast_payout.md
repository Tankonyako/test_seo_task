 [img: Fast-Payout Time and KYC requirement]

The overall payout speed depends on two factors: the casino’s internal processing time and the transfer time of the chosen payment method. Depending on the combined value, Canadian casinos can be grouped into the following categories:

*   **Instant:** Sites with the fastest cashout time will let you get a payout in under 1 hour, which we explain in detail on the dedicated page. This type is rare, and you may need to have a verified profile to ensure this speed.
*   **Same-day:** Casinos with 24-hour withdrawals are more common. As a rule, it takes the operator a few hours, after which you need to wait for your method’s processing time.
*   **Fast:** Payouts reach your account within 1 to 3 business days, often because operators manually review requests for security and fraud prevention.
